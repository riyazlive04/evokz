Evokz Console

What each part of the dashboard does, and how to run image generation on your own fal.ai account.

What Evokz does

Evokz writes, designs and delivers social media posters for your clients — automatically, every day, on a schedule you set once. You add a client and pick their plan; Evokz does the rest and sends each finished poster straight to their WhatsApp.

How one poster gets made

This runs on its own, every day, at each client's set time.

1
The clock reaches the client's delivery time. Evokz picks up today's entry from that client's content calendar.
2
The background image is generated. This is the step that costs money, and the only step your fal.ai key pays for.
3
The poster is designed. Evokz lays the client's logo, colours, headline and contact details over the image.
4
It is saved to Google Drive, in that client's own folder.
5
It is sent to the client on WhatsApp, with the caption and hashtags. The entry is marked Delivered.

If anything goes wrong

The day is marked Failed and the reason is written next to it in plain words on the dashboard. Nothing is silently skipped, and nothing half-finished is ever sent to a client.

The tabs across the top

Dashboard Your home screen. Today's numbers, costs, failures, and what is going out next.
Plan Your campaign packages — name, how many days it runs, and the price you charge.
Verticals The industries you serve. Each client belongs to one, which steers their creative style.
Clients Every client, their campaign dates, and their daily delivery time.
Posters A free preview of every poster layout. Costs nothing — use it to show people the range.
Demo A practice client for sales calls. Nothing here ever sends on its own.

Section by section

The Dashboard, top to bottom

1  Four counters

At a glance

Active clients · Due today · Delivered · Failed. Click any one to see exactly which clients are behind that number. If Failed is not zero, start there.

2  Spend

Money

What the automation is costing you, in rupees, over the period you choose at the top. It splits the cost by service and by client, and shows your margin — the plan fee minus what that client actually cost to run. Set a monthly cap on a client and Evokz will flag them here when they go over it.

3  Failed deliveries

Only when needed

Appears only when something did not go out. Each one shows the reason in plain words. Retry failed tries them all again; where the poster was already made, it re-sends that same poster instead of paying to make a new one.

4  Event queue feed

What's next

The next posters due to go out, across all clients, with a thumbnail of each. This is your check that tomorrow morning is covered. Two buttons on each card:

Send now Sends the poster that already exists, straight away. Free — nothing is re-made.
Regenerate Throws away the image and makes a brand new one. This costs money — use it when the client did not like the picture.

5  Image generation key

Your fal.ai account

At the very bottom. This is where you put your own fal.ai key, so that image generation is billed to you rather than to Evokz. Full instructions on page 4.

A yellow strip near the top

If you ever see a yellow bar listing settings in capital letters, something the system needs has not been filled in and that part will not work. Send a photo of it to your technical contact — it is not something to fix from the dashboard.

Two words worth knowing

Content calendar The list of what each client gets on each day of their campaign — the topic, the caption, the hashtags, and the description used to make the picture. It is written once, up front, and you can edit any day before it goes out.
Vertical The client's industry — real estate, construction, and so on. It shapes how their posters look and read. Each client has exactly one.

Section by section

The other tabs

Plan

Your packages — for example “100-Day Blitz”. Each has a name, a number of days, and the full price you charge for the whole campaign. The number of days decides when a client's campaign ends. The price is what the dashboard uses to work out your margin, so it is worth filling in. A package cannot be deleted while clients are still on it — Evokz will tell you how many.

Verticals

The industries you serve. Add one before you can add a client in it. Open a vertical to upload up to 20 reference posters — examples of how posters in that industry should look. These are a design library for your team; they do not change what the system produces today.

Clients

Every client in one table. From this screen you can:

Change delivery timeClick the time, type a new one, press Enter. Escape cancels.
Pause or resumeA paused client receives nothing until you switch them back on.
Open a clientClick the company name for their full detail page.

Inside a client you will find their campaign progress, their brand page (logo, colours, tagline, phone and website that appear on every poster), the option to upload a calendar you wrote yourself instead of having it generated, and the controls to delete the client or clear their unsent days.

Posters

Every poster layout, side by side, at whatever size you pick. This is free and uses no credit at all — the pictures are made on your own machine. Refresh it as often as you like. It is the right screen to show a prospect what their posters could look like.

Demo

A practice client built for live sales calls: set up a fake company, generate a real poster, and it arrives on WhatsApp immediately so the prospect sees the whole thing work. It is excluded from the daily schedule, so it never sends anything on its own. Generating here does cost money, exactly like a real client.

The one rule worth remembering

Sending an existing poster is free. Making a new picture costs money. So Send now and Retry failed are safe to use freely; Regenerate and the Demo tab spend real credit each time.

Billing

Using your own fal.ai key

fal.ai generates the background pictures. By default Evokz pays for it — add your own key and every picture is billed to you instead.

Part 1 — Get your key from fal.ai

1
Go to fal.ai and create an account, or sign in to your existing one.
2
Open Billing and add a payment method or top up. A key with no money behind it will not make pictures.
3
Open Settings → API Keys (on some screens it sits under your account menu, top right).
4
Click Add key / Create key. Give it a name like “Evokz” so you know later what it is for.
5
Copy the whole key immediately — a long code with a colon in the middle; copy both sides of it. fal.ai shows it only once. If you lose it, delete that key and make a new one.

Part 2 — Put it into Evokz

1
Open the Dashboard and scroll to the bottom, to Image generation key.
2
Paste the key into the box. Add a label if you like — just a note to yourself about which account it is.
3
Click Test key first. This makes one small picture to prove the key works — it costs under a rupee and changes nothing. Wait for the green tick.
4
Click Switch to my fal.ai key. A yellow warning appears; click the red button to confirm. From the very next poster onward, fal.ai bills you — nothing to restart, no waiting.

This is a one-way switch

You can swap in a different key of your own at any time — just paste the new one and save. But the dashboard cannot hand billing back to Evokz. If you need that, ask your technical contact; it takes them under a minute.

To change your key later

Same place, same steps: paste the new key, Test key, then Save key. No second confirmation is needed.

If posters stop going out

What you seeWhat to do
“fal.ai rejected the key” Cancelled, mistyped, or out of money. Check your fal.ai balance, then save a working key.
“cannot be decrypted” Something changed on the server. Paste your key in again and save — that fixes it.
A yellow bar in the key section The server is missing a one-time setting. Send it to your technical contact.

Evokz will never quietly pay on your behalf. If your key stops working, posters stop and the dashboard says why.
