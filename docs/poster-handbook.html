Evokz Poster Handbook

Everything you need to run the posters: what to type, where to type it, and what to do when something looks wrong.

01 What this does

Every client gets one poster a day, sent to them on WhatsApp.

You write the words in a spreadsheet. The system finds a photo and draws the poster. You look at it and approve it. It sends at the scheduled time.

Your three jobs

Fill the sheet. Check the posters. Approve them. Everything else runs on its own.

02 What changed

Recent fixes worth knowing about, because they change what you can expect.

  • Posters now match their design exactly. What you see in the design sample is the shape the client gets — same layout, same icons, same colours.
  • The client's logo now fills the space it is given. It used to draw tiny. It also used to turn black on dark designs and vanish; it doesn't now.
  • You decide how many feature boxes appear. Write two, get two. The row closes up neatly instead of leaving gaps.
  • A row with only one feature box no longer loses everything else. It used to silently throw away that row's headline, sentence and button text, and replace them with words nobody wrote.
  • The poster maker recovers on its own. If it stops working it now restarts itself, instead of failing every poster for every client until someone stepped in.

03 The flow

  1. Add the clientName, phone number, logo, website, tagline, and how many days the plan runs.
  2. Choose the verticalMedicals, Constructions or Interiors. This decides which designs are available.
  3. Fill the sheetOne row per day. Section 4 explains every column.
  4. Import itThe console shows you a preview before anything is saved. Check it, then import.
  5. The posters are madeA photo is bought and the poster is drawn. This takes a minute or two per day.
  6. You approve each oneLook at the poster. Happy — approve. Not happy — regenerate.
  7. It sendsOn WhatsApp, at the scheduled time. Approved posters only.

The two people always get wrong

The bar in the headline
BUILT TO LAST|FINISHED ON TIME makes two lines. Without the bar it is one long line and the design will squash it.
The accent line number
This picks which headline line is coloured. Write 2 and the second line is coloured. It is not a count.
The dash

Some cells come pre-filled with a dash: —. That design has no room for it. Leave it alone — anything you type there will not appear on the poster.

Rules

  • Don't rename a design. Every sheet still using the old name stops working.
  • Don't put phone numbers or websites in the sheet. They come from the client's own settings, so they are right on every poster automatically.
  • Don't go past the plan length. A 7-day plan takes 7 rows. Extra rows are refused at import.
  • Don't ask for text or logos inside the photo. The design puts the words on top; text in the picture collides with them.
  • Fill a feature box completely or not at all. A heading with no sentence under it is refused, and the message tells you which box.

06 Seeing the posters

Go to Clients, open the client, and scroll to Upcoming calendar entries. Each card shows the finished poster, the date it sends, the design used and the caption.

The buttons on each card

Approve
You are happy with it. It will send at its scheduled time.
Send now
Sends immediately, without waiting for the schedule.
Regenerate
Makes the poster again from scratch, with a new photograph.
Delete
Removes the day entirely. It will not send.
Open in Drive
The full-size image, in Google Drive.
Nothing sends unapproved

A poster you have not approved will not go out, however long it sits there. Approving is the only thing that releases it.

07 When something looks wrong

What you seeWhat it meansWhat to do
Some rows say INVALID when importing Usually the sheet has more days than the plan allows. Use the button offering to import the days that fit, or lengthen the plan first.
A message naming a column That one cell is too long, empty, or half-filled. Fix that cell and import again. The message says which row and which column.
The poster looks wrong, or the photo is odd The photograph came out badly, or the words don't suit it. Click Regenerate. If it is the words, fix the sheet and import again.
No logo on the poster Either the client has no logo uploaded, or the design has no logo space. Check the client's settings first, then section 5.
An error mentioning browser on one card A hiccup while drawing that one poster. Click Regenerate. It should work the second time.
Every poster failing Something is wrong with the platform itself. Tell the developer. This is not something to fix from the console.

08 Known quirks

Things the system does that will confuse you. They are known, and none of them stops a poster going out.

  • The feature icon column does nothing. Each design has its own fixed icons. Whatever you type in those cells is ignored — write anything, or leave it.
  • Con-SM-3, Con-SM-5 and Con-SM-6 always show a person. Even if you describe a hard hat, cement bags or a digger, you will get someone standing.
  • Con-SM-2 prints the word "Visit" with nothing after it when the client has no website saved. Leave the website label blank for those clients.
  • Con-SM-3 draws a gold line with nothing under it in the same situation.
  • Five Medicals designs never show a logo — Med-SM-11, 12, 13, 15 and 16. Use a different design for clients whose logo matters.

09 What needs a developer

These cannot be done from the console or the sheet. Asking for them will save you a day of trying.

  • A new design, or changing how an existing one looks.
  • New icons, or changing which icon a design uses.
  • Moving anything on a poster — the logo, the phone number, the boxes.
  • Changing colours or fonts.
  • Adding a new vertical.
  • Any case where every poster is failing at once.
